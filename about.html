Hello! I'm Aerry.

I'm a Malaysian tech professional with a unique background blending design, quality assurance, and product delivery.

My Journey

My career began in quality assurance, where I learned to think critically about user experience and system reliability. Over time, I discovered my passion for design and how it intersects with testing and product development. This multidisciplinary approach allows me to understand products from multiple perspectives—ensuring they're not just beautiful, but functional and trustworthy.

Why Design + QA?

Design is about solving problems for people. My QA background taught me attention to detail, edge case thinking, and user empathy. Combined with my growing design skills, I create experiences that are intuitive, reliable, and delightful. I believe that the best products are built when design and quality work hand in hand.

What I'm Passionate About

I'm interested in creating digital experiences that help people accomplish their goals effortlessly. Whether it's through thoughtful interface design, comprehensive user testing, or collaborative problem-solving, I aim to make technology more accessible and human-centered.
