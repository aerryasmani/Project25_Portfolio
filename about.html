My Journey So Far

I enjoy creating digital experiences that are clear, reliable, and easy to use. My journey started with User Interface design, where I focused on simplifying user flows and making products more accessible for non-tech-savvy users.

Over time, my curiosity led me beyond design into systems, support, and testing. Exploring how products work behind the scenes naturally pulled me toward quality and automation.

Today, I work as an Automation Tester, combining my UX background and system understanding to help build user-friendly and dependable products.

Why Design + QA?

My interest in IT began with my late grandmother, who often struggled to use mobile apps. Seeing how poor usability could exclude elderly users pushed me toward human-centered User Interface design.

User Interface design shapes how people interact with products, while QA ensures those interactions work reliably My background as an engineering student trained me to pay attention to detail, edge case thinking, and user empathy. The best products are built when empathy and quality work hand in hand.

I want technology to feel usable for everyone; regardless of age or familiarity with modern systems
